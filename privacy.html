Asaumi – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Asaumi is a private app used by two people only. It is not offered to the public. This page explains what data the app uses and why.

What the app stores

  • Account: the email address used to log in (Firebase Authentication).
  • Content you create: chat messages from the last 10 days, memories, wishes, profile name and settings (Google Firebase / Firestore).
  • Photos, videos and voice notes: stored in Cloudinary; only their links are kept in the app.
  • On your phone: your full chat history, saved locally in the app.
  • Location (optional): only if you turn on "Together", and only while the app is open.

Google Drive

If you choose Back up to Google Drive, the app asks Google for the drive.file permission. With it, Asaumi can only create and read the files it made itself (one chat backup file in a folder called "Asaumi backup"). It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Google Drive. The backup is used only to restore your own chat on your own phone.

Sharing

Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers or used for anything other than running the app for its two users. It is visible only to the two accounts of the app (Firestore security rules), except "personal" wishes, which only their creator can see.

Deleting your data

You can delete messages, memories and wishes inside the app. To remove the Drive backup, delete the "Asaumi backup" folder in Google Drive, and you can remove Asaumi's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. For anything else, contact the email below.
